## E9 🎛️ The platform app + `control-service` (operator console v0) — ACTIVE (born 2026-09-30, design session; reshaped 2026-10-15, D31)

**Since** 2026-09-30 · **Decisions** D24, D30, D31, D32, D33 · **Manual** —

**Mission:** the platform app and its control plane. One front end, `web/platform`, serves every platform page; the operator console is its **role-gated admin area**, not a separate app (D31 — amends D24 R1/R4). Behind it one Spring Boot service, **`control-service`** — the control plane to `market-data-service`'s data plane: it owns the `control` database (users, roles, jobs, settings), holds the read model over the `market_data` data product (read-only creds, D17) + R2, sends the digest and alerts (D30), and later carries the control actions and the kill switches execution needs; Spring's earning moment is here and nowhere else.
